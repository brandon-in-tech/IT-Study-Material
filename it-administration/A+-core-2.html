Welcome to Core 2

Core 2 tests what support technicians do after hardware has been deployed: manage operating systems and accounts, recognize and mitigate security problems, systematically troubleshoot software, and follow reliable service procedures. This is a learning aid, not an official CompTIA product.

Exam at a glance

220-1202: Up to 90 multiple-choice and performance-based questions, 90 minutes, passing score 700 on a 100–900 scale. Confirm scheduling details with CompTIA before booking.

How to use this course

Study one domain at a time, mark completed lessons, then use flashcards for recall. Attempt practice questions before reading explanations. Complete safe labs on personal or authorized machines and save redacted screenshots in your portfolio.
