Eight-week study plan

  1. Weeks 1–2: Windows, macOS, Linux, OS installation, devices, management.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Security, permissions, MFA, endpoint protection, encryption, identity and response.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Software diagnosis, startup, drivers, logs, applications and mobile incidents.
  4. Week 7: Operational procedures, documentation, recovery, safety, and all guided labs.
  5. Week 8: Revisit weak lessons, complete mixed scenario exams, and practice performance-based workflows.
