How to study effectively

  1. Read the lesson and explain the decision in your own words.
  2. Work through the example, then answer its scenario before expanding another lesson.
  3. Check off a lesson after you can explain it without notes.
  4. Use flashcards for retrieval practice rather than passive reading.
  5. Take practice questions and analyze each explanation, including wrong choices.
  6. Complete authorized labs in isolated personal VMs or simulations, never on production assets.

Eight-week preparation roadmap

  1. Week 1: Security concepts, control types, CIA, IAM and MFA. Start 15 flashcards daily.
  2. Week 2: Cryptography, certificates, secure protocols, common attacks and social engineering. Analyze fabricated phishing messages.
  3. Week 3: Malware, application attacks, network threats, vulnerabilities and remediation priorities. Create a risk-based patch plan.
  4. Week 4: Zero trust, enterprise segmentation, cloud security, backup, RTO/RPO and data protection. Draw a resilient service design.
  5. Week 5: Security operations, telemetry, SIEM, detection, incident lifecycle, evidence and response. Build your incident playbook.
  6. Week 6: Endpoint protection, email security, automation, hunting, governance, audits and third-party risk.
  7. Week 7: Complete all labs, revisit weakest exam domains, make a one-page formula/acronym sheet, and explain case-study decisions aloud.
  8. Week 8: Take timed practice exams and PBQ-style scenarios, remediate knowledge gaps, and focus on interpreting questions rather than keyword guessing.
