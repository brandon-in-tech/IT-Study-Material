Eight-week Network+ roadmap

  1. Weeks 1–2: OSI, TCP/IP, Ethernet, IP subnetting, IPv6, DNS/DHCP, transport and wireless fundamentals.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Configure and verify VLANs, trunks, STP, routing, gateway redundancy, WAN and enterprise Wi-Fi labs.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Monitoring, telemetry, cabling, documentation, QoS, security architecture, certificates and change control.
  4. Week 7: Work systematically through troubleshooting labs; interpret captures, counters and CLI output.
  5. Week 8: Complete mixed quizzes, revisit missed concepts, practice scenario decisions and compare with official N10-009 objectives.
